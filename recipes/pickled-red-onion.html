Pickled Red Onion

Home

Description

This is one of the most useful pickled condiments. I like to eat with Mexican food, but it fits well in many culinary applications.

Ingredients

Servings: 3 cups

  • 1 red onion
  • 1 1/4 cups distilled white vinegar
  • 1 1/4 cups water
  • 2 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 birds eye chilis
  • 2 bay leaves

Steps

  1. Thinly slice onion from root to stem
  2. Bring water, vinegar and salt to boil
  3. Pour over onion and aromatics into glass jar
  4. Close jar and refrigerate once it cools sufficiently

Notes

  1. Ideally, boil the jar for 10 minutes in advance to sterilize it. This will lengthen the pickles' lifespan in your fridge. The lid can just be washed with warm soapy water.
