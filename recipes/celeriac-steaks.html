Celeriac Steaks

Home

Description

These are insanely good. If you've never had celeriac before, please start here. This recipe made me fall in love with it.

Ingredients

Servings: 3-4

  • 1 celeriac
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Avocado oil

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400
  2. Peel the celeriac (easier with a knife) and slice it into 1 inch rounds
  3. Rub it with oil, salt and pepper and roast, flipping after 25 minutes or so

Notes

  1. Inspired by this recipe: https://www.thevegspace.co.uk/celeriac-steak/
  2. Goes well topped with an herby lemony dressing / gremolata / sauce
